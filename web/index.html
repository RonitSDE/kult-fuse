    How a mandate works

    1. Commit

      The owner writes the curve, the cap, the kill line and the expiry to a Fuse account on Solana. Its hash is the policy.

    2. Propose

      An agent or deterministic rules engine proposes exposure from DFlow/Kalshi data. Stale, wide or thin quotes cannot justify more risk.

    3. Enforce

      The program rejects any target off the curve, above the cap, after expiry or after a kill. Only then does the worker trade on Velocity.

    4. Prove

      The system records venue fills and attempts to anchor receipts. Only independently matched transactions should be described as verified.

# KULT Fuse Devnet pilot

Invite participants to one featured Commitment Vault with a clear question and a published resolution source. Share its direct link. Ask participants to return after launch, trade the side they actually expect to win and follow the result through claims.

Capture three separate measures for the run.

| Measure | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Adoption | Testers invited, unique active participants where known, and participants who returned after launch |
| Market formation | Finalized vault creation, participant commitments and launch receipts |
| Trading and settlement | Finalized Fuse swaps, distinct trading addresses, repeat addresses, test mUSDC volume, observed DAO balances, resolution and a winning claim |

Keep team smoke wallets identified separately from community participants. Wallet counts measure signed addresses. Group participation by wallet only where the participant chooses to share that mapping.

Start the operator run with [the Devnet handoff](PREDICT_V2_WINNING_HANDOFF.md). Record outcome and activity after the run.
